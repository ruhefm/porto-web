# Sunda

The region currently known as West Java (Jawa Barat) holds a rich historical legacy that deserves recognition beyond its modern administrative label. This area was once the heartland of the Sundanese kingdoms, including **Tarumanagara**, **Galuh**, and **Pakuan Pajajaran**.

If Indonesia is truly committed to preserving its diverse cultural heritage, then acknowledging and honoring local identities through place names is an important step. Renaming or co-naming regions with their indigenous historical names can strengthen cultural pride and awareness.

For instance, the province of **West Java** could also be referred to as **Pasundan**, echoing its historical and cultural roots. Likewise, **Sunda Kalapa**, the old name for what is now part of Jakarta, was once a thriving port of the Sunda Kingdom. Today, it is somewhat ironic that Sundanese cultural identity has faded in this very place — where Javanese honorifics like _Mas_ are now more commonly used.

To foster a truly inclusive national identity, we should promote the use of **neutral or locally appropriate terms** when addressing others, respecting Indonesia’s linguistic and cultural diversity.
